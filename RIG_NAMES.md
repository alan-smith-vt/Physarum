# Rig names

The lidar / Pi rigs are named after stars, in alphabetical order (chosen 2026-11-03). The name goes on the front
panel below the SGH logo, and is the rig's Pi hostname (lowercase).

| # | Name | Star | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Alnilam** | middle star of Orion's Belt | Arabic *al-niẓām*, "the string of pearls" |
| 2 | **Bellatrix** | Orion's left shoulder | Latin, "female warrior" |
| 3 | **Castor** | one of the Gemini twins | |
| 4 | **Dabih** | in Capricornus | Arabic, "the slaughterer's lucky star" |
| 5 | **Elnath** | the bull's horn, in Taurus | Arabic, "the butting one" |

Spare / next: **Capella** (in Auriga; Latin, "little she-goat").

Rig 1 (Alnilam) is the v4 core printed 2026-11-02. Its Pi is still hostname `lidarpi` until renamed.
